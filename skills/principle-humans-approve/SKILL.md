# Humans approve

Agents propose. People approve what prospects read and what the brain believes.

**Why:** one bad message to a real buyer costs more than a month of manual work, and a wrong belief in `context/` poisons everything after it.

Research can be automatic. Messages and context changes are drafts until a human approves them. Anything irreversible is done by a human.
