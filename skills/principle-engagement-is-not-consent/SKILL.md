# Engagement is not consent

A like is a signal, not permission.

**Why:** signals tell you someone may care. They don't create a legal basis, and they don't settle the rules for cold email in a given country.

Respect exclusions and opt-outs before anything else. Collect only what a play needs, and record where the data came from. When the law in a market is unclear, stop and ask a human.
