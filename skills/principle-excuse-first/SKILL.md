# Excuse first

No honest reason, no message.

**Why:** GTM runs on excuses. A good excuse makes a message useful to the reader, and the excuse type is what the loop learns from.

A real excuse is true, recent, and about them. If any of the three fails, don't send. Don't dress up a pitch as a reason, and don't imply a relationship or an introduction that didn't happen.
