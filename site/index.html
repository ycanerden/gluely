The GTM brain for your agent

Gluely keeps what your agent needs to sell well: who you sell to, how you write, which plays work, and every correction you have made. Your agent does the thinking. Gluely remembers, checks the work, and learns.

It is open source and runs on your machine. Keep the tools you have, bring your own data and keys, and connect any agent through the command line or MCP. Nothing is sent and nothing is spent until a person says yes.

How it works

The company is different every time. The loop is the same, and every turn of it makes the next draft better.

  1. Your agent learns the company, and argues with it

    Run /setup-gluely. Your agent maps your stack, reads your real deals with your permission, and challenges your ICP before it writes anything down.

    # example, with made-up numbers
You said mid-market. Your deals say otherwise:
7 of 9 won deals had under 30 people.

Who fits your ICP perfectly and never buys?

  2. Plays are set up one question at a time

    Pick a classic. Gluely asks about each step, shows your options and saves your answers, each play with a kill rule. Press the answers to try it.

  3. Nothing leaves without a check

    Gluely works out the bill from your own prices before anything spends a credit, and a missing price is never counted as free. Lists are sorted before they leave, so customers and excluded companies are held back. Your agent can read the estimate, but only a person can approve it.

    $ gluely cost job-switch
# made-up prices
Check the list   $4.12
Find contacts    $0.06 to $0.12

Total            $4.18 to $4.24
A person needs to approve it.
    $ gluely check company rival.example
✘ excluded: competitor

$ gluely check draft --channel email \
  --text "I hope this finds you well"
✘ banned phrase

  4. Every correction teaches it

    Every decision and every edit is logged, with who made it and why. Your agent reads those lessons before it writes the next draft. When you keep changing the same thing, Gluely shows you, and you can turn it into a rule.

    $ gluely learn
voice  3 open, belongs in context/messaging/voice.md
  I never use exclamation marks
  Changed "Quick question" to "One question"

You keep removing  "quick" 2x

  5. Every lesson becomes a test

    Early reply rates are mostly noise, but your corrections are not. Each one can become a test, and every change to the context is tested against the last one, so you see it when the brain gets worse.

    $ gluely test
Brain tests   context 319e1d3
✔ Rule tests   4/4
✔ Agent tests  6/7 correct
  ✘ icp-warm-founder: answered hot, expected warm

Who it's for

One architecture for every kind of team. Each brain stays private to the company it belongs to.

  • Founders selling alone

    Write down who you sell to, then let your own deals argue with it. Start with the people who already know you.

    gluely plays start own-network

  • GTM engineers

    Keep the stack you have. Gluely adds the rules between the tools: who is excluded, what a play costs, and which tool really finds the right address.

    gluely waterfall contact-data

  • Freelancers and agencies

    One private brain for each client, never mixed. Each one learns that client's voice, and you take your method from one to the next.

    gluely init ~/client-brain

  • Product-led apps

    A trial ending, a cancel or a downgrade each gets a note that fits the moment, in the right voice for the address.

    gluely plays start product-lifecycle

Use any tool

Gluely provides no data. Bring whatever you already pay for. The library lists tools by the job they do, in alphabetical order, and ranks nobody. Your own tests rank your tools.

    Questions

    Does Gluely send emails or messages?

    Only when a person says yes. Gluely never calls a model: your agent drafts, and Gluely checks the draft. A play's workflow can send through the tool you already use, but only after a person approves the rows, in a terminal.

    Does Gluely see my data?

    No. Your brain is a folder on your machine, kept in a private repo you own, and Gluely has no servers. Your agent sees what you connect to it, so connect only what you would show that agent's provider, and keep connections read-only.

    Which agents work?

    Any agent that can run commands or connect to an MCP server. Claude Code and Cursor get the skills as slash commands. Codex, Grok and others read the same skills as plain markdown files.

    Where does the data come from?

    From your own tools and accounts. Gluely provides no data and resells nothing, so you can use any provider you like and test them against each other.

    What does it cost?

    Gluely is free. Your data tools charge what they charge, so Gluely works out the bill from your own prices before anything spends a credit, and only a person can approve it.

    Is it ready?

    It is an early preview. The brain works today: context, guided plays, the customer guard, cost checks, results, the decision log, tests and the MCP server. Signals, the person database, replies and CRM sync come next.

    Your brain stays yours

    Your context, plays and tests live in your own private repo. Gluely has no servers, so your data stays in your own stack. It is free, MIT licensed, and recommends tools without ever requiring one.
