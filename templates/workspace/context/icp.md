# ICP

A rubric your agent can check from a title and a company record. Not an essay.

## Must have
Any miss scores 0.
- <must-have criterion>

## Strong fit
+30 each, capped at 60.
- <strong signal>

## Weak fit
+10 each.
- <weak signal>

## Disqualifiers
Any match scores 0.
- <disqualifier>

## Scoring
0 if a must-have fails or a disqualifier matches. Otherwise 40 + strong + weak, up to 100.
Hot is 70 or more. Warm is 50 to 69. Ignore is below 50.
